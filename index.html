What is in here

Every listing on this dashboard is an entry-level IT support, help desk, desktop, SOC, cloud, NOC or systems role in Maryland, DC/Virginia, Texas, or 100% remote nationwide, checked to be open at the time of the refresh. Roles that are above entry level get left out rather than listed with a lower score - a short honest list beats a long padded one.

Reading a job card

The search profile behind the scores. B.S. Information Technology, Towson University (Dec 2025) - CompTIA Security+ and Cloud+ - clearance-eligible. Baltimore / DMV area. Entry-level help desk, desktop support, SOC, cloud, NOC and systems administration roles across Maryland, DC/Virginia, Texas and 100% remote nationwide, spanning commercial, state and federal employers. Strengths: help desk and desktop support, Windows and Active Directory fundamentals, networking and security fundamentals, and cloud fundamentals (Security+ and Cloud+ certified).

The number in the ring is an overall fit score out of 100. Green is a strong fit (80+), amber is good with a gap (70-79), orange is a stretch (60-69).

The badges tell you the things that decide whether you can apply today: whether it needs a security clearance, which of your certifications (Security+, Cloud+) the role matches, which market it is in, and which sector the employer sits in.

Clearance-eligible is good news, not a barrier. It means the employer will sponsor the clearance - you do not need to hold one already.

Click Details on any card to see why it scored what it did, what is known about the employer, and a notes box that saves as you type.

Saving jobs and emailing them to yourself

Every card has a Save to pipeline menu - choose Interested, Applied, Screening, Interview or Offer to save the job. Choose Save to pipeline again to remove it.

The bar at the top of the Pipeline tab then works on everything you have saved:

  • Email to me - opens your email app with the list already written out, grouped by stage, with links and your own notes. You just press send.
  • Copy list - the same text on your clipboard to paste anywhere
  • Download CSV - a spreadsheet for Excel or Google Sheets
  • Backup and Restore - see the warning below

Your saved jobs survive the daily refresh. Each job is remembered by employer and title rather than by its position in the list, so a job you marked Applied stays Applied tomorrow even though the list around it changes.

Your saved jobs live in this browser only. They are not stored on a server. If you clear your browsing data or switch to another computer or phone, they will not follow you. Use Backup now and then, and Restore to load them back.

New jobs and finding your place

Jobs added since your last visit carry a New badge, and the line above the cards says how many there are. Choose Sort: new since last visit to put them first. Once you scroll past the filters, a search bar stays pinned to the top of the screen; Filters takes you back to the full set.

Gap analysis and tailored resume

Every card has two links, Gap analysis and Tailor resume:

  • Gap analysis - compares the posting with your resume: what you match, what's missing, which gaps are hard barriers (like an active clearance) and which you can close with a lab project or course, and whether it's worth applying.
  • Tailor resume - rewrites your resume for that posting, using only what's already in it. Anything the job asks for that you don't have is listed separately instead of being added.

Each link prepares a prompt with that job's details. Click Copy prompt, then open whichever AI assistant you use - ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot or another - start a new chat, attach your resume, paste and send. Your resume and the results stay in your own chat - nothing is posted to this page. Always read a tailored resume before you send it.

The Pipeline tab

Everything you have saved, laid out as a board with one column per stage. The arrows under each card move a job forward or back as you progress. Your notes travel with the job.

What the tiles mean

  • Open roles - the total on the dashboard right now
  • High fit - scored 80 or above, apply to these first
  • Maryland, DC / Virginia and Texas - where they are
  • No clearance - roles with no clearance requirement at all
  • In pipeline - how many you are tracking

How to actually use this

Apply to five to seven roles a week and tailor each one - that beats firing off thirty identical applications. Start with the High fit roles that need no clearance and no certification, because those have the shortest path from application to interview.

Use the notes box on each card to record who referred you, which version of your resume you sent, and when to follow up. Then email yourself the list at the end of the week so you have a record outside the browser.

What this dashboard does not know

Employer details are incomplete for many listings, and those fields say so rather than guessing.

Plenty of entry-level roles are never posted publicly - they come through career services, professors, alumni and referrals. Treat this as one input to your search, not the whole map.
